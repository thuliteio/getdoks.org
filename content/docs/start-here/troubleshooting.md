This page helps you find answers to common problems with Thulite.

## Verify your setup

Use the [Settings](/docs/reference/configuration/settings/), [Parameters](/docs/reference/configuration/parameters/), and [Page Frontmatter](/docs/reference/configuration/page-frontmatter/) reference pages to confirm your configuration is correct.

## Consult the documentation

If you cannot find an answer in this documentation, visit the [Thulite Docs](https://docs.thulite.io/thulite/) for framework guidance, and refer to the [Thulite SEO Docs](https://docs.thulite.io/seo/), [Thulite Images Docs](https://docs.thulite.io/images/), and [Thulite SVG Docs](https://docs.thulite.io/svg/) for integration-level guidance.

## Ask the community

Check for known [issues on GitHub](https://github.com/thuliteio/doks/issues), or ask for help in [Thulite Discussions](https://github.com/thuliteio/doks/discussions). The community is active and happy to help.
